## 4/6. AG meeting at the Bologna WLCG/HSF workshop 2026

Reminder - we had a 2nd 2026 AG meeting during the Bologna workshop to (1) avoid having a long 1-year delay (as in the past) while (2) profiting from the fact that most AG members will be at the workshop anyway.

Key points raised:
- Discussion on scheduling the next meeting: pros and cons of co-scheduling with the WLCG/HSF workshops. No conclusion as yet but decisions expected soon on timing and location of the next meeting.
    - Pros and cons since some representatives were taken by sessions at the workshop, whereas it was for sure nice to meet in-person when possible.
- General comment made from the SG side that further engagement from the AG is welcome.
    - It will for example help making sure new PhD students and newcomers in general are made aware of the HSF early enough for them to engage.
    - Seminars - successful, attendance varies a lot, help in advertising the seminars would be appreciated, see point just above.
- Expand the set of Engaged Communities hence the AG composition?
    - JUNO was pointed out, a WLCG member not (yet) included.
    - What about Dark Matter / direct detection experiments?
- Affiliation programme:
    - Updated strategy - available online - with a standard lightweight review (requirements following best practices) was very well received.
    - A differential review remains possible, but only on a case-by-case basis, to be discussed with the SG.
- HSF "work strategy":
    - AG advocates for a task force based approach - the HSF could stand up task forces with a very well defined scope/task.
    - Does this imply a change to how Activity Areas are defined and work?
    - Before setting the 2027 AA conveners it would be worth considering whether or not to discontinue some groups in case there appears to be no demand for them? They could be reactivated if the demand changes of course, or depending on Tasks considered.
    - HSF states it’s a balancing act: we want to encourage younger people and make sure the community contributing to the HSF has a home too.

DISCUSSION + ACTIONS:
- Agreed to move to concrete year goals for AAs.
    - For those AAs with no concrete task force, can still specify concrete HSF tasks such reviewing projects for HSF affiliation, organising seminar topics.
- We will organise an on-boarding meeting early in the year.
- Ask the AG for ideas of tasks for AAs to take up in 2027.

### Engagement within HSF bodies and with the community

**Commitment within members:**
- Several AAs are very quiet, hardly engaging.
- The SG members need to lend a hand.
    - It's too often the same colleagues over and over again taking up tasks.

**Engagement community-HSF:**
- We need to better communicate with/to the outside world, meaning the community.
    - The AG should play a role here, serving as liaison to Engaged Communities (i.e. experiments, community projects) other bodies.
    - The HSF is not brought to the attention of new PhD students and early-career colleagues as nearly much as it could. This is a missed opportunity. More engagement would be beneficial to all.
    - How to make the HSF and the Forum mailing list (more) widely known within the community?
