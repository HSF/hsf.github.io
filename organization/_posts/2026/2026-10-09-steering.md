#### "AI Statement" for contributors to HSF repositories

So far HSF repositories do not provide any statement on AI-powered/-helped contributions, and we are getting some regularly. On occasions we can be flooded by not-to-useful contributions. This year an “AI statement” is strongly suggested in GSoC proposals. We should do something similar. 

- There is an ongoing discussion at <https://github.com/HSF/hsf.github.io/issues/1919> and there are 2 open PRs to review asap:
- <https://github.com/HSF/hsf.github.io/pull/1953>: addition of CONTRIBUTING.md.
- <https://github.com/HSF/hsf.github.io/pull/1949>: addition of PULL_REQUEST template.
- Both have been up for long time with no input other from 2 people.
    - ACTION: please review next week and we will then go ahead and merge (can get follow up PRs if needed).
    - (Note: the PRs are not exclusively about "AI contributions" - also include good etiquette, due diligence, etc.).
